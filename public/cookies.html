1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and provide a better experience.

  • Essential Cookies: Needed for the website to function (e.g., storing your session or consent).
  • Analytics: Using Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site so we can improve it.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense uses cookies to show you relevant ads and measure их performance.
  • User Preferences: Remembering that you've accepted our terms and conditions.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can choose to block cookies or receive a notification when a cookie is set. However, blocking all cookies may affect your experience on our site.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Please note that third parties (including ad networks and analytics providers) may also use cookies, over which we have no control.
